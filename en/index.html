The apartment

Quiet, bright and close to the sea

The 65 m² apartment is in a family house in Jagodje, a quiet residential area above Izola. It suits 2 to 4 guests.

Bedroom

Two single beds, wardrobe, door to the balcony.

Living room

Sofa and extendable bed for 1–2 people, dining table, TV, door to the large balcony.

Kitchen

Fridge, freezer, dishwasher, toaster, coffee maker, dining corner.
